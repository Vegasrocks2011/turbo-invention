The question
Evaluate the use of genetic engineering / selective breeding / genetic screening in modern biology.

Conclusion — you must make a judgement
On balance, the benefits of __________ outweigh / do not outweigh the risks because __________. However, this is only true provided that __________
The conclusion must do more than say "there are benefits and risks." It must state which side is stronger and explain why. That is what "evaluate" means.

Conclusion
One benefit of genetic engineering to produce herbicide-resistant crops is that farmers can spray the whole field with herbicide, killing weeds but not the crop. This reduces competition for water, minerals and light, increasing crop yield. A further advantage is that higher yields mean more food can be produced on the same area of land, which is increasingly important as the global population grows.

However, there are significant risks. The herbicide-resistance gene could spread to wild plants through cross-pollination, producing herbicide-resistant weeds that are very difficult to control. This could also reduce biodiversity, as efficient weed removal eliminates plants that other organisms depend on. There are also ethical concerns — some people believe it is wrong to alter the genetics of organisms and release them into the environment when the long-term consequences are not fully understood.

The extent to which gene transfer is a problem depends on the proximity of wild relatives of the crop plant and how carefully the technology is regulated.

On balance, the benefits of GM herbicide-resistant crops outweigh the risks, particularly their potential to address global food insecurity. However, this judgement holds only provided that robust regulation is in place to prevent uncontrolled gene transfer and monitor long-term ecological effects.
Notice: the conclusion does not just say "both sides have merits." It makes a clear judgement, states a condition, and explains the reasoning. That is a 6/6 conclusion.
Use this to self-assess your answer before you hand it in.
1–2 marks
One or two points made with no development. No reasoning. No conclusion.
"GM crops are good because they help farmers. But they might be bad for the environment."
3–4 marks
At least one benefit and one risk, both explained. A conclusion is present but only restates the two sides without weighing them.
"...Overall, there are both benefits and drawbacks to GM crops."
5 marks
Two benefits and two risks, all explained with reasoning. Conclusion makes a clear judgement — states which side is stronger.
"On balance, the benefits outweigh the risks because..."
6 marks
All of the above plus: conclusion explains why one side is stronger, acknowledges conditions or limits, uses precise scientific vocabulary throughout.
"On balance, the benefits outweigh the risks, particularly regarding food security — provided that regulation prevents uncontrolled gene transfer."
Self-check before you finish: Have I written "because" at least three times? Have I written "on balance"? Have I named a specific risk and a specific benefit — not just "there are risks" and "there are benefits"?
